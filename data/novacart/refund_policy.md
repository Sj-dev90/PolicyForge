# NovaCart Customer Returns & Refund Policy

Document Version: 1.0
Domain: Customer Order Fulfillment & Restitution

## Section 1: Standard Eligibility Windows
[RP-1] Customers may request a refund within 30 days of delivery for non-final sale items.
[RP-2] Refund requests submitted more than 30 days after delivery are strictly denied.
[RP-3] Final sale items are not eligible for refunds under any circumstances.

## Section 2: Pre-Delivery & Delivery Prerequisites
[RP-4] Refunds cannot be processed before an item is delivered; cancel the order instead.
[RP-5] Physical return receipt at our distribution center is required to finalize monetary disbursement.

## Section 3: Loyalty Tiers & Extended Windows
[RP-6] VIP members enjoy an extended 60-day refund window.
[RP-7] Standard customers cannot return opened non-damaged items after 14 days.

## Section 4: Damage & Order Modifications
[RP-8] Items reported as damaged during transit are eligible for refund review within 45 days.
[RP-9] If a replacement order is cancelled, the customer may request a refund.
[RP-10] Promotional shipping fees and expedited courier charges are non-refundable.
