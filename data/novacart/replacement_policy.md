# NovaCart Product Replacement Policy

Document Version: 1.0
Domain: Defect Rectification & Item Exchanges

## Section 1: General Replacement Terms
[REP-1] Items may be replaced within 30 days of delivery.
[REP-2] Replacements cannot be requested after 30 days from delivery.
[REP-3] Final sale merchandise cannot be replaced.

## Section 2: Defective and Damaged Goods
[REP-4] Damaged goods qualify for replacement up to 45 days after delivery.
[REP-5] Customers must document packaging or item damage prior to exchange authorization.

## Section 3: Operational Constraints
[REP-6] Only one active replacement request is permitted per order.
[REP-7] VIP members are eligible for replacement processing up to 60 days from delivery.
[REP-8] Opened merchandise that is non-defective cannot be exchanged after 14 days.
[REP-9] Cross-category exchanges for different product types are strictly prohibited.
