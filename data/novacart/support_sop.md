# NovaCart Customer Support Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

Document Version: 1.0
Domain: Tier-1 & Tier-2 Customer Escalation Handling

## Section 1: Verification & Intake
[SOP-1] Agents must verify order delivery status before creating return authorizations.
[SOP-2] Refunds cannot be processed before an item is delivered; cancel the order instead.

## Section 2: Cancellation & Exception Handling
[SOP-3] If a replacement order is cancelled, the customer may request a refund.
[SOP-4] Final sale items are not eligible for refunds under any circumstances.
[SOP-5] Damaged goods qualify for replacement up to 45 days after delivery.

## Section 3: Priority Overlaps & Loyalty Discretion
[SOP-6] For VIP customers reporting transit damage within 45 days, agents are authorized to approve a replacement.
[SOP-7] VIP members enjoy an extended 60-day refund window.
[SOP-8] Standard customers cannot return opened non-damaged items after 14 days.
[SOP-9] Only one active replacement request is permitted per order.
[SOP-10] Chargeback dispute cases must be frozen; never disburse refunds while a bank dispute remains open.
